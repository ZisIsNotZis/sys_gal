# v2 Domain Glossary

- **World:** authoritative objective state and event scheduler; mindless with
  respect to narrative meaning.
- **Event:** an objective occurrence committed by the world, an actor action,
  or a seeded world rule.
- **Perception:** private observations available to one actor at a time.
- **Belief:** an agent-owned proposition and confidence; never an objective
  world field.
- **Memory:** agent-owned retained or distorted experience.
- **Intention:** a concrete attempted action, not a desired plot outcome.
- **Affordance:** a legal action shape derived from current state and access,
  not a story option.
- **Trajectory:** the complete saved sequence of perceptions, intentions,
  outcomes, and objective events.
- **Seed:** initial objective facts, rules, agents, schedules, and discoverable
  information.
- **System:** the one seeded supernatural mechanism, constrained by explicit
  rules and unable to author human emotions.
- **Novel layer:** later prose selection and arrangement over a trajectory.
- **Game layer:** later player-facing action selection learned from sampled
  trajectories.
