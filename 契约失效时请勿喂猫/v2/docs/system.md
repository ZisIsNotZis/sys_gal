# The Ledger (v2 seed specification)

The Ledger is the only supernatural mechanism. It is bound to Chen because he
made three incompatible promises without defining scope. It is proud, literal,
and genuinely interested in precise obligations. It is not a narrator, quest
director, moral judge, romance meter, or plot controller.

It can offer a contract, observe objective events in its operating area, and
answer one narrow question about an eligible named document or recorded
statement after a contract condition is satisfied. It cannot read private
thoughts, force another actor, make someone attend, reveal an unearned secret,
or classify affection.

Its comedy comes from literal system behavior. For example, it may ask whether
repeated voluntary co-presence should be recorded as an obligation. Chen may
accept or reject; another person is not bound by his answer.

The case is an explicit lifecycle: `offered -> accepted -> completed`, or
`offered -> declined`. Acceptance records only the terms and the condition;
it does not grant anything. A correct, objective fact query is the seeded
condition that emits `system_answer` followed by `system_reward_granted`, with
the answer event as evidence. A voluntary decline emits the seeded
`system_penalty_applied` consequence. Invalid queries consume no quota and
emit no reward or penalty. All of these events are committed through the
world's `commit_external` boundary and are private to Chen. The meaning of
the case and all human reactions belong to the System and human agents, not
to the neutral world kernel.
