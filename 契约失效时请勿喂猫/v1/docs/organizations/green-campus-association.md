# Green Campus Association

## Purpose

Student organization focused on environmental practice and practical campus improvements. For the fair, it is raising money for tactile wayfinding signs and accessible route improvements at three nearby schools.

## Relevant people

- [Lin Yao](../characters/lin-yao.md), treasurer.
- [Amani Njoroge](../characters/amani-njoroge.md), volunteer accessibility-map coordinator.
- [Park Min-seo](../characters/park-minseo.md), dashboard maintainer.

## Internal culture

Members genuinely care about the project but disagree about process. Some prioritize winning the grant window; others prioritize accurate reporting even if the fair is delayed. The association is short on money and volunteer time.

## Vulnerability

The grant requires a credible financial plan and an accessibility implementation schedule. A failed fair does not automatically destroy the project, but it makes the next review much harder.
