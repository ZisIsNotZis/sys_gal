# Luming University (鹿鸣大学)

## Identity

Private comprehensive university in Haicheng with approximately 18,000 students. Its public mission is innovation, civic engagement, and international exchange.

## Internal reality

The university is a collection of offices with different incentives. Student affairs values safety and reputation; finance values traceability; community partnerships values external relationships; academic departments value schedules and graduation outcomes. No office has a complete view of the fair.

## Relevant units

- **Office of Student Affairs:** supervises student organizations and event compliance.
- **Community Partnerships Office:** processes the fair permit through [Luo Wen](../characters/luo-wen.md).
- **Student Union:** controls much of the event's logistics and vendor coordination.
- **Green Campus Association:** runs the accessibility fundraising project.
- **Campus Newspaper:** an independent student publication with a small editorial budget.
- **International Student Office:** supports exchange students but has limited authority over event planning.
- **Campus Security Office:** controls after-hours access, incident reports, and some CCTV retention.

## Institutional habits

Written responsibility matters more than good intentions. Offices often answer only the exact question asked. A student can receive three individually reasonable answers that do not combine into a complete process.

## Knowledge boundaries

The university knows the permit status and submitted paperwork. It does not automatically know private drives, unsubmitted drafts, informal conversations, or student motives.
