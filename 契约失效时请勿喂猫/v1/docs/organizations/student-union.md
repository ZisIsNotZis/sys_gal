# Luming Student Union

## Purpose

Student representative organization responsible for coordinating campus events, clubs, vendors, volunteers, and much of the Spring Community Fair's operational paperwork.

## Relevant people

- [Qiao Shun](../characters/qiao-shun.md), vice chair and external-partnership lead.
- [Gao Rui](../characters/gao-rui.md), logistics officer.

## Incentives

The union needs a successful fair, a clean annual review, and favorable recommendations for senior officers. It is not a criminal organization. Its recurring weakness is informal delegation: tasks are handed over in chat messages and clarified only when something goes wrong.

## Records

The union's shared drive contains the original payment export, visible reconciliation sheets, version history, volunteer rosters, and vendor correspondence. Access rights are broad enough that mistakes can be hidden temporarily but narrow enough that unusual access may leave a log.
