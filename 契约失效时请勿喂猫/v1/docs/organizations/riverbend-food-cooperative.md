# Riverbend Food Cooperative

## Identity

A small food business supplying prepared food and temporary stalls to local events. It grew from Xu Meiling's family restaurant near the old river market.

## Relevant people

- [Xu Meiling](../characters/xu-meiling.md), owner-manager.
- Xu's younger brother, Xu Jian, delivery coordinator; not yet a primary agent.

## Incentives and limits

The cooperative has eight regular workers and a seasonal loan. It needs predictable deposit timing and does not want a university dispute attached to its name.

It will negotiate hard and use urgency, but it will not knowingly falsify university records. Staff may independently make mistakes while handling deliveries, receipts, and phone calls.
