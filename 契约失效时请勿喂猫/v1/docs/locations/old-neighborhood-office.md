# Closed Neighborhood Committee Office

## History

A former neighborhood committee building in Qinghe district. Its basement flooded during the 2013 typhoon blackout and was renovated in 2014. The building is now used for archive storage and occasional community workshops.

## Relevant objects

An old storage cabinet contains a blue rabbit umbrella and a red plastic whistle from the 2013 storm. The cabinet is locked but the key was duplicated during renovation and one copy is in a mislabeled facilities envelope.

## Present access

The building is not a forbidden ruin. It is normally accessible to facilities staff and authorized community groups. Students may enter during a workshop or by asking a staff member, but wandering into archive storage is suspicious.

## Narrative function

The location can provide physical evidence for the childhood incident, but evidence does not force a correct memory. A person finding an object must still identify it, connect it to the past, and decide whether to disclose it.
