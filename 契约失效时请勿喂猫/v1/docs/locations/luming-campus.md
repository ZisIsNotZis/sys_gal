# Luming University Campus

## Overview

The main campus sits between an older residential district and a new commercial avenue. It is walkable but large enough that crossing campus costs meaningful time, especially in rain.

## Relevant places

- **Dormitory 3:** Chen Mo and Gao Rui's residence; Room 417; a ground-floor utility corridor has an unlocked lost-property cabinet.
- **Library:** Chen's workplace; quiet, monitored, and open later than most offices.
- **Student Union Building:** shared printers, meeting rooms, and the union's document workstations.
- **Community Partnerships Office:** Luo Wen's office; walk-ins end at 18:00.
- **Greenhouse Courtyard:** association meeting area, partly covered, with poor wheelchair access after rain.
- **North Gate bus stop:** frequent bottleneck during rain.
- **Riverside market road:** route to Riverbend Food Cooperative and the old neighborhood.

## Perception constraints

Walls, doors, rain, crowd noise, lighting, line of sight, and attention all matter. CCTV is not omniscient: coverage is incomplete, retention is limited, and retrieving footage requires a valid request.
