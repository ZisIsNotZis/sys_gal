# Old Riverside Market

## Overview

A mixed residential and commercial district with food stalls, repair shops, a pharmacy, a small ferry landing, and narrow covered walkways. It is busier than campus but less formally observed.

## Relevant places

- **Riverbend kitchen:** Xu Meiling's cooperative kitchen and loading point.
- **Lantern Bridge:** a covered pedestrian bridge with a view of the river and frequent student foot traffic.
- **Seventeen Steps Café:** where Park Min-seo works evening shifts.
- **Qinghe Community Clinic:** ordinary clinic used by residents and students.

## Social texture

Local residents recognize regular students but do not know their campus roles precisely. Rumors travel through shop owners and delivery workers, often losing dates and names.
