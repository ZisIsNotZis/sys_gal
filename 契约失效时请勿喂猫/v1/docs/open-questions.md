# Open Design Questions

These are deliberately unresolved. Later simulation experiments should teach us what the harness needs instead of prematurely forcing a plot.

## Character and story questions

- What exactly did Lin hear through the basement pipe during the storm?
- Does the red whistle still work, and who finds it first?
- Why did the Ledger's legacy cat clause become prominent?
- Is Xu Meiling's bank-facing summary merely aggressive accounting or a contract violation?
- What does Qiao Shun know about earlier student-union shortcuts?
- Does He Qian publish before or after she verifies Gao's account?
- Romance distribution for the next update is fixed in [romance-arc.md](romance-arc.md).
- Can Chen tell the truth without turning truth-telling into another way of avoiding responsibility?

## Simulation questions

- Which routine activities need exact procedural modeling in v1?
- What minimum evidence packet lets an agent distinguish “I saw it” from “I inferred it”?
- Which social actions can use deterministic outcome rules, and which need semantic adjudication?
- How should the harness represent partial listening, ambiguous wording, and delayed understanding?
- How much of a character's long-term memory should be loaded into each wake packet?
- How should the Ledger phrase a case without accidentally revealing objective facts Chen has not earned?
- What is the smallest event history a semantic resolver can receive while preserving causal correctness?

## Diversity expansion

The current core cast includes local and non-local students, an international exchange student, a mature graduate student, an administrator, a small business owner, different socioeconomic pressures, and different relationships to institutional power. Future additions should broaden lived experience without making identity itself a plot shortcut. Possible additions include a commuter with family care duties, a security worker with local knowledge, and a disabled student who is not defined solely by accessibility advocacy.
