# Red Plastic Whistle

## Objective facts

A cheap red emergency whistle bought by Chen's aunt in 2013. Chen carried it during the storm and blew it after reaching the convenience store. Its sound may have been what Lin heard through the basement pipe, but no surviving record proves this.

It is in the same storage cabinet as the blue rabbit umbrella. The whistle still works, but its volume is weak because the internal pea is warped.

## Evidence value

The whistle supports Chen's account that he sought help, but it does not prove how long he was gone or what Lin experienced.
