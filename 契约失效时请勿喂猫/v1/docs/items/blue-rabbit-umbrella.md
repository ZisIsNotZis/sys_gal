# Blue Rabbit Umbrella

## Objective facts

A child-sized blue umbrella with two faded rabbit stickers and a bent metal rib. It belonged to Lin Yao during the 2013 storm and was left in the closed neighborhood committee basement.

It is currently in the old storage cabinet described in [old-neighborhood-office.md](../locations/old-neighborhood-office.md). The handle has a small bite mark from a dog, not a manufacturing defect.

## Knowledge and evidence

Lin remembers owning an umbrella but not where it went. Chen remembers the color vaguely and does not know it survived. The object itself cannot identify its owner to an ordinary person.

## Possible interactions

It can be found, recognized, repaired, returned, photographed, or used as a misleading clue. None of these actions automatically restores either character's full memory.
