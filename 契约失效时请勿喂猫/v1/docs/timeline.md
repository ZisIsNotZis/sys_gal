# Objective Timeline

This file records what actually happened. It is not a list of facts every character knows.

## Before 2026

- **2004-11-02:** Xu Meiling is born in Haicheng to a family running a small restaurant near the old river market.
- **2004-12-18:** Luo Wen is born in a county town several hours from Haicheng.
- **2004-08-09:** Chen Mo is born in Haicheng.
- **2004-03-27:** Lin Yao is born in Yongzhou, an inland city, and moves to Haicheng with her mother at age eleven.
- **2003-07-14:** He Qian is born in Nanjing. Her family later relocates repeatedly because of her father's work.
- **2004-09-30:** Gao Rui is born in Haicheng.
- **1998-06-21:** Qiao Shun is born in Haicheng. He will later become vice chair of the student union.

## Childhood: the storm incident

- **2013-08-17, 19:12:** A typhoon warning causes a sudden blackout in the Qinghe residential district.
- **2013-08-17, 19:20:** Nine-year-old Chen Mo and nine-year-old Lin Yao take shelter in the unlocked basement office of a closed neighborhood committee building. Chen has a small red plastic whistle; Lin has a blue rabbit umbrella.
- **2013-08-17, 19:28:** Water enters the basement corridor. Lin panics and bangs on a pipe.
- **2013-08-17, 19:31:** Chen leaves through a side stairwell to find an adult, after promising Lin he will return quickly.
- **2013-08-17, 19:43:** Chen reaches a convenience store and brings back Chen's aunt, who calls emergency services.
- **2013-08-17, 19:51:** Lin is found. She is physically safe but has vomited from fear. Her blue umbrella is left behind in the office.
- **2013-08-18:** Chen's aunt tells Chen not to discuss the incident because Lin's mother is upset and the adults are handling it.
- **2014-02:** The building is renovated. The blue umbrella and red whistle remain in a locked storage cabinet, unnoticed.

Neither child receives a complete explanation. Lin's strongest memory is Chen leaving after promising to stay. Chen's strongest memory is being unable to make the adults move faster. Their memories become emotionally stronger and factually less complete over time.

## Fair and accounting history

- **2025-11-06:** Xu Meiling's company, Riverbend Food Cooperative, agrees to provide food stalls for the 2026 fair. The contract contains a deposit-return clause and a confusing cancellation timetable.
- **2026-01-12:** Lin Yao's Green Campus Association receives provisional approval for an accessibility fundraising project linked to the fair.
- **2026-02-04:** Qiao Shun asks Gao Rui to migrate old vendor spreadsheets into a new template before the student union audit.
- **2026-02-18:** A vendor cancels one order. Gao Rui enters a refund twice while reconciling a late-night payment export.
- **2026-02-19:** Gao Rui notices a total mismatch, corrects the visible sheet, and deletes an intermediate backup because he fears the mess will be misunderstood. The original payment export remains in cloud storage.
- **2026-02-26:** Xu Meiling asks Qiao Shun whether the cancellation clause can be represented more favorably in a bank-facing cash-flow summary. Qiao says he will “see what can be done” but does not promise anything in writing.
- **2026-03-02:** Qiao asks Gao Rui to prepare two versions of the vendor summary: the formal university version and a preliminary internal version. This is normal only if clearly labeled; Gao labels neither clearly.
- **2026-03-05:** Lin notices that the reimbursement total in a shared folder does not match the treasurer's ledger. She makes a private copy before asking questions.
- **2026-03-07:** He Qian receives a message from Gao: “The numbers are not clean. Don't publish yet.” She interprets it as possible fraud but does not know the details.
- **2026-03-09:** Luo Wen's office suspends final fair-permit processing and requests a corrected vendor reconciliation, a deposit explanation, and named signatories.
- **2026-03-11:** Gao gives Chen an outdated form and asks him to keep it temporarily. Chen says, “I'll handle it,” without asking what handling means.
- **2026-03-12:** Chen tells Luo he will bring the corrected paperwork, tells Lin he will find out why the permit is suspended, and tells Gao he will not let him take the blame alone. These are three different commitments.
- **2026-03-13:** Chen finds the old form in his backpack, notices the duplicate-looking line, and avoids opening the cloud link Gao sent him.
- **2026-03-15, 22:10:** The Ledger binds to Chen after it detects three overlapping, semantically incompatible commitments.
