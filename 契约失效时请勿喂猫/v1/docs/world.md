# World: Haicheng and Luming University

## Overview

Haicheng (海城) is a prosperous second-tier coastal city in southeastern China. It has a humid subtropical climate, a large port, several universities, older riverside neighborhoods, and a growing software-and-logistics economy. Spring is rainy and windy; sudden showers are common in March.

The story takes place in 2026. Ordinary physical, social, legal, and economic reality applies. There is no public supernatural activity. The Ledger is the only confirmed supernatural mechanism in this version.

## Social texture

Haicheng contains multiple overlapping communities rather than one uniform student culture:

- local students who live with parents or commute;
- students from inland provinces living in dormitories;
- international and exchange students navigating language and bureaucracy;
- graduate students and adult learners with jobs and family obligations;
- disabled students and accessibility advocates;
- student clubs with different levels of money and institutional access;
- vendors, delivery workers, administrators, security staff, and residents affected by university events.

People have limited time, unequal resources, different communication habits, and reputations that persist beyond a single conversation. Help is possible but not free: it costs time, trust, money, face, energy, or future reciprocity.

## University

Luming University is a private comprehensive university with about 18,000 students. Its public image emphasizes innovation, civic engagement, and international exchange. In practice, its offices are busy, its procedures are fragmented, and informal promises frequently outrun written approvals.

The university has no single conspiracy controlling the fair. Different offices optimize different risks. Students often interpret procedural friction as personal hostility because they cannot see the workload and liability behind it.

## Technology and communication

Characters use smartphones, group chats, campus apps, email, QR payments, shared documents, and ordinary social media. Read receipts, message previews, forwarded screenshots, deleted messages, weak Wi-Fi, battery levels, and notification settings can matter.

A message being sent does not mean it was read. A message being read does not mean it was understood. A screenshot may omit context or metadata. Public group messages and private messages have different social consequences.

## Supernatural rules

The Ledger is bound to Chen Mo. It is real, consistent, and capable of effects unavailable to ordinary people, but its powers are narrow and contractual. It cannot rewrite history, create matter from nothing, possess people, directly alter affection, or make an impossible physical event happen.

## Ensemble episode contract

The first complete ensemble episode runs on 2026-03-16 from 07:00 to 18:00.
Nine embodied participants are active: Chen, Gao, Lin, He Qian, Luo, Qiao,
Xu, Amani, and a three-legged stray cat. Their arcs are independent: the
accounting dispute, Qiao's audit risk, Xu's cash pressure, He Qian's editorial
verification, and Amani's rain accessibility failure intersect through
documents, messages, deadlines, and ordinary proximity. The cat is not a
mascot controlled by the Ledger; it can expose an object by ordinary behavior,
but cannot speak, contract, or resolve a human conflict.

The terminal event is authoritative and explicit. Every major participant must
have a recorded outcome (cooperate, refuse, publish, defer, leave, or remain an
animal with no human obligation). No character is required to be satisfied:
the episode is complete when each has a defensible consequence and the central
case has settled or failed.

The following are seed invariants:

- institutions respond to documents, deadlines, incentives, and liability;
- people protect information when disclosure threatens something they value;
- a good intention does not erase a bad consequence;
- attraction does not imply trust, compatibility, availability, or consent;
- embarrassment changes behavior only through a character's psychology and circumstances;
- secrets have discoverable evidence, even when the evidence is ambiguous;
- coincidences are ordinary and bounded by geography, schedule, and opportunity;
- characters can fail to notice what is visible if attention, knowledge, or expectation makes that plausible;
- no one becomes incompetent merely to give Chen Mo an opportunity.
