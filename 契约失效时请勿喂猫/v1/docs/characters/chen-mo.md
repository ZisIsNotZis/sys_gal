## Public presentation

Polite, adaptable, observant, and easy to ask for favors. He is not naturally charismatic. People approach him because he appears to absorb inconvenience without protest.

## Actual behavioral engine

Chen learned early that usefulness can stop an argument. When adults are disappointed or upset, he offers a practical solution before he understands the emotional or logistical problem. Saying “I'll handle it” produces immediate relief and postpones the moment when he must admit uncertainty.

His strongest desire is not success; it is to remain the person nobody regrets relying on. His strongest fear is being exposed as someone who accepts responsibility to escape discomfort rather than from genuine courage.

He is capable of lying by omission, especially when he can describe the omission as “waiting until I know more.” He dislikes direct emotional conversations but is good at noticing concrete details.

## Objective childhood history

See [timeline.md](../timeline.md), the storm incident of 2013-08-17. Chen left Lin Yao in the basement to find an adult and returned with his aunt. He was told not to discuss the incident. He has never learned that Lin interpreted his departure as abandonment.

## Accessible memory

Chen remembers:

- being trapped with Lin during a blackout;
- promising to stay;
- leaving because he thought no adult would hear them otherwise;
- returning to find adults already handling the situation;
- feeling that his return was too late.

He does not remember the exact time he was gone or Lin's reaction when he returned. He remembers the red emergency light and the sound of water more clearly than words.

## Unconscious patterns

- When someone becomes distressed, he searches for an object, form, route, or person who can solve the problem.
- When someone asks “Will you stay?” he tends to answer with a practical promise.
- He becomes unusually restless in rooms with flickering red light or the smell of wet concrete.
- He dislikes blue rabbit motifs without knowing why.
