# Relationship Map

Relationships are directed and time-dependent. “Trust” is not one universal number. A character may trust someone's competence while distrusting their honesty, or feel attraction while avoiding dependence.

## Chen Mo ↔ Lin Yao

Childhood neighbors and former playmates who have not had a real conversation in years. Lin remembers Chen leaving during the storm. Chen remembers failing to help quickly enough.

Current pattern: Lin demands specificity; Chen offers practical action instead of emotional explanation. This produces mutual irritation with dormant familiarity.

Hidden pressure: the blue rabbit umbrella and red whistle can reactivate the childhood history, but neither object automatically restores a correct memory.

## Chen Mo ↔ Gao Rui

Roommates and friends for eighteen months. Chen knows Gao is careless under deadline pressure. Gao knows Chen can be induced to accept work by framing it as a small favor.

Current pattern: mutual loyalty mixed with unequal honesty. Gao asks for protection; Chen promises before understanding the cost.

Breaking point: if Chen reveals Gao's error without acknowledging his own concealment, Gao may see it as betrayal rather than honesty.

## Chen Mo ↔ He Qian

Acquaintances through library work and campus articles. He Qian has interviewed Chen because he gives unusually precise descriptions of mundane events.

Current pattern: He Qian finds him useful but evasive. Chen finds her questions invasive but respects her preparation.

Romantic possibility: attraction can emerge through intellectual friction and reliable disclosure, but her journalistic incentives can conflict with his privacy.

## Chen Mo ↔ Luo Wen

Student-administrator relationship. Luo Wen considers Chen polite and potentially dangerous in the specific way of students who promise before reading instructions.

Current pattern: she gives him opportunities to clarify; she will not compensate for missing documents by guessing his intent.

## Chen Mo ↔ Amani Njoroge

They know each other through library and fair logistics. Amani sees Chen as unusually willing to notice small access barriers, but also as someone who accepts work without defining it.

## Chen Mo ↔ Park Min-seo

Practical acquaintances. Min-seo has corrected Chen's spreadsheet formatting and knows he is embarrassed by technical mistakes. Chen finds Min-seo difficult to read and sometimes mistakes their boundaries for dislike.

## Lin Yao ↔ He Qian

Lin wants accurate reporting but fears a premature article will damage the accessibility project. He Qian sees Lin as a valuable source who is withholding evidence.

## Lin Yao ↔ Gao Rui

Treasurer and logistics officer. Lin sees a pattern of sloppy documentation. Gao sees Lin as someone who will turn an error into a formal investigation.

## Gao Rui ↔ Qiao Shun

Junior officer and vice chair. Qiao recruited Gao partly because Gao is willing to do tedious work. Gao believes Qiao will protect him; Qiao has not made that promise.

## Luo Wen ↔ Xu Meiling

Administrator and vendor. Luo sees a business under pressure; Xu sees a procedural gatekeeper who may determine whether her employees are paid on time. Neither is secretly corrupt, but each has incentives to present facts selectively.

## Amani Njoroge ↔ Lin Yao

Amani respects Lin's persistence but believes Lin sometimes turns a genuine shared goal into a personal test of reliability. Lin values Amani's specific criticism and is afraid of disappointing her.
