# Romance Arc Plan for the Next Update

This is the v2 design target, not a claim about the current v1 run.

Romance is an independent causal thread, not a reward for solving the
accounting case. Attraction, admiration, dependence, jealousy, trust,
resentment, and consent are separate directed states. The Ledger cannot create,
measure, or reward love.

## Romantic pressure

- Lin Yao has restrained attraction toward Chen mixed with childhood hurt,
  distrust, and active boundary-testing. She controls her own route and may
  approach, pause, or refuse.
- Chen Mo's attachment is initially disguised as responsibility. He must learn
  to state what he wants without turning intimacy into an obligation.
- He Qian has playful intellectual curiosity and possible mild attraction, but
  remains primarily an interviewer protecting evidence. She is ambiguity, not
  an automatic rival route.
- Gao Rui has poorly expressed, possibly unrequited admiration for Qiao Shun.
  It may influence loyalty and embarrassment but never excuses his error or
  compels Qiao to reciprocate.
- Qiao notices the asymmetry and avoids naming it because of hierarchy and
  reputation.
- Amani and Xu have explicit independent romantic boundaries; neither is route
  bait. Park may have a private crush outside the cast, but it is not a prize.
- The cat is never anthropomorphized romantically. The Ledger may misclassify
  territorial behavior as “exclusive attachment” for comedy.

## Main route

```text
Chen wants closeness -> expresses it as useful obligations
-> Lin detects pressure and withdraws
-> Chen chooses vulnerability or convenient usefulness
-> Lin decides whether closeness is safe
```

The storm is not proof of destined love. Both childhood memories are partial;
intimacy requires acknowledging harm without demanding absolution.

## Required v2 structure

Add at least four player-facing choices and six substantive romance scenes:
an awkward investigation encounter, a failed interview-that-is-not-a-date, a
confrontation about the old promise, the storm-memory conversation, a boundary
conflict caused by the Ledger, and a final consensual meeting or separation.

Choices must change observable directed states such as trust, warmth, reliance,
resentment, communication willingness, and consent status. Lin must initiate
meaningful actions and may refuse Chen even when he is helpful.

The Ledger may propose: “Repeated voluntary co-presence detected. Proposed
category: mutual obligation.” It can help define a meeting or boundary, but
cannot make Lin attend, forgive, or love Chen.

## Valid romance endings

1. Cautious beginning: one mutually agreed, uncontracted meeting.
2. Warm distance: attraction is acknowledged but both delay a relationship.
3. Honest separation: Lin declines and Chen accepts without bargaining.

The rendered VN must display the ending caused by prior choices, never a
default confession.
