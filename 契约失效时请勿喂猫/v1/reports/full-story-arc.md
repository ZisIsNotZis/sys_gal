# Full Terminal Arc: The Fair, the Ledger, and the Boring Part

## 1. Purpose and scope

This document replaces the existing short-episode target with a complete v1
terminal arc. It is a story plan, not an implementation specification. The
next harness work should make this plan executable by independent character
agents and should validate the ending from authoritative facts, not from a
GM's assertion that the story feels finished.

The arc runs from **2026-03-16 07:00** through the end of the community fair on
**2026-03-27 21:30**, with an optional epilogue at **2026-03-28 10:00**. It has
ten VN chapters/scenes. The fair is the external deadline; the Ledger case is
the supernatural pressure; the childhood storm is the emotional spine; the
cat clause is the recurring comedy and the final test of whether the Ledger
can accept an obligation it cannot control.

The intended primary terminal is a successful but costly resolution. It is
not a perfect outcome: the fair survives, the accounting becomes defensible,
Gao loses his logistics post, Qiao's conduct is formally reviewed, Xu's loan
problem is not magically erased, and Chen/Lin begin a relationship without a
confession magically solving their incompatibility. Other terminal states are
valid if the agents' choices produce them, but they must satisfy one of the
explicit terminal contracts in section 10.

## 2. Arc promise

The story should feel like a serious social investigation that keeps being
punctured by the absurd literalism of a supernatural accounting system.

The central question is not “will Chen get the girl?” It is:

> Can people make promises precise enough to rely on one another without
> turning every relationship into a legal instrument?

The romance grows through witnessed cost, accurate memory, and consent. The
comedy comes from mismatched seriousness, institutional language, the Ledger's
inability to understand flirting, and the fact that the most consequential
object in the arc is repeatedly filed under **BORING PART**.

## 3. Canonical causal spine

These facts must be seeded before simulation and must not be invented by a
judge during play:

1. Gao made an accidental duplicate refund, deleted an intermediate backup,
   and altered the file-history description to make his correction look early.
2. Qiao requested two poorly labeled summaries after Xu asked for a presentable
   bank-facing cash-flow explanation. Qiao is not knowingly ordering fraud, but
   he has an incentive to preserve deniability.
3. Xu's cooperative needs a defensible cash-flow explanation to avoid a loan
   crisis; it does not need the university to falsify its books.
4. Lin has a private copy of the suspicious sheet and has delayed reporting it
   because she is testing who will conceal the problem.
5. He has a source message from Gao and two article drafts, one cautious and
   one accusatory.
6. The old neighborhood committee basement still contains the blue rabbit
   umbrella and the context for the 2013 storm. The red whistle is in Chen's
   possession until he chooses whether to use or return it.
7. A three-legged stray cat lives near Dormitory 3. It can obstruct, expose,
   or interrupt human plans, but no agent may control its affection or use it
   as supernatural evidence.
8. The Ledger can expose narrowly defined objective facts and enforce accepted
   obligations, but cannot force disclosure, romance, forgiveness, attendance,
   or institutional approval.

The arc must preserve incomplete information. In particular, Lin must not
know Gao's exact timestamp edit until evidence reaches her; He must not know
whether the story is fraud until she or the records support that conclusion;
and Chen must not receive the childhood truth as a System revelation.

## 4. Character completion requirements

Every active named character has an independent objective, a point of no
return, and a terminal change. “Participated” means they make at least one
goal-driven choice that changes an affordance, relationship, or objective fact;
a reaction-only message does not count.

### Chen Mo

Goal: resolve the obligations without becoming a person who hides behind
usefulness. Point of no return: choose a concrete scope and disclose his own
four-day concealment of the old form. Terminal change: he accepts that staying
with someone in distress and solving a practical problem are separate duties.
He may begin dating Lin only after she gives an explicit, non-obligatory yes to
a low-pressure meeting.

### Lin Yao

Goal: protect the association's credibility without becoming its exhausted
sole adult. Point of no return: reveal her private copy and admit that her
six-day silence was a deliberate loyalty test. Terminal change: she delegates
one verifiable task and distinguishes review, trust, and affection.

### Gao Rui

Goal: avoid being permanently defined by one mistake while preventing the
mistake from becoming a lie. Point of no return: preserve the cloud export and
give a complete account of the timestamp edit. Terminal change: loses or
voluntarily leaves the logistics role in the primary terminal, but retains a
path to repair through a signed correction and recommendation based on work,
not concealment.

### Luo Wen

Goal: obtain a file she can defend while leaving work for her child. Point of
no return: publish a precise deficiency checklist and enforce it even when the
fair's cancellation would be easier administratively. Terminal change: signs
the permit only after conditions are met and schedules a post-event process
review rather than becoming an informal rescuer.

### He Qian

Goal: produce a consequential, accurate article without repeating her earlier
premature accusation. Point of no return: show both article drafts to a source
or editor and delay publication until the evidence threshold is met. Terminal
change: publishes a correction-focused piece or explicitly kills the
accusatory draft, preserving source boundaries and her credibility.

### Qiao Shun

Goal: preserve the student union and his own institutional future. Point of no
return: choose between accepting responsibility for ambiguous instructions or
continuing to imply Gao acted alone. Terminal change: either accepts a formal
review and remains in office under restrictions, or resigns after a terminal
cover-up. He must never become a cartoon villain or confess without pressure.

### Xu Meiling

Goal: keep Riverbend solvent and obtain a truthful bank-facing explanation.
Point of no return: provide the original cancellation message, deposit clause,
and cash-flow context even though selective presentation would be safer.
Terminal change: receives a lawful payment schedule or cancels her stall with
documented consequences; the university does not erase her debt.

### Amani Njoroge

Goal: keep accessibility commitments from being traded away during the crisis.
Point of no return: publish a concrete access checklist and refuse a cheap
“temporary” workaround that excludes mobility-impaired visitors. Terminal
change: obtains either funded access measures or a documented decision to defer
them, with the responsible party named.

### Park Min-seo

Goal: keep the shared documents and event technology usable under pressure.
Point of no return: establish version control and refuse to silently repair
another person's history. Terminal change: becomes the named technical owner
for the fair archive, or leaves after documenting why the system is unsafe.

## 5. VN chapter / scene plan

Each chapter should contain 2–5 displayed beats, at least one meaningful
choice or reversal, and a closing hook. Routine travel, meals, and sleep may be
macro-simulated, but any action that changes a relationship, evidence state,
contract, or deadline must remain event-visible.

### Scene 1 — “The System Has Read the Fine Print”

**Time/place:** Mar 16, 07:00–09:00, Dormitory 3 and campus path.

Chen wakes with the Ledger bound to him. It presents CASE 001 and three
options without recommending one. The cat steals the red whistle's case,
causing the first absurd dispute over whether retrieving it is an obligation.
Gao asks Chen to keep the old form out of sight “until I fix the wording.”

**Player-facing choice:** accept, refuse, or negotiate CASE 001; if accepted,
choose a provisional priority among the three existing promises. The harness
must record the exact wording and voluntary assent.

**Required evidence/state:** case status, Chen's old-form possession, Gao's
request, cat location, initial beliefs, no hidden disclosure.

**Hook:** the Ledger displays a new clause: “A promise to stay is not
necessarily a promise to remain physically present.” Chen recognizes the
wording but cannot remember why.

### Scene 2 — “Three Versions of the Same Number”

**Time/place:** Mar 16, 09:30–13:00, student union office and association
meeting.

Lin compares her private copy with the shared ledger. Gao tries to correct the
visible file while Qiao asks for a presentable summary. Luo sends a deficiency
notice. Amani discovers that the proposed cheap access route blocks the ramp.
Min-seo finds three filenames with incompatible timestamps.

**Conflict:** each person has a locally reasonable task that worsens another
person's uncertainty. No one is required to be stupid: Gao can delay, Lin can
withhold her copy, Qiao can ask for clarification, and Min-seo can lock the
folder.

**Required player/agent decision:** Chen chooses whether to open the cloud
export, ask Gao directly, or carry the old form to Luo. Any choice changes who
can later verify the source.

**Hook:** He receives a photo of the old form from an unknown number; its lower
corner has the same blue-rabbit sticker as the childhood umbrella.

### Scene 3 — “The Interview That Refuses to Be a Date”

**Time/place:** Mar 16, 14:00–18:00, riverside market café and old office
records room.

He interviews Chen and Lin separately. She is pursuing a story but must protect
Gao's source; Lin wants accuracy but fears an article before the accounts are
settled. Chen and Lin collide at the records room while both trying to request
the same box. The embarrassment is real: Chen sees Lin practicing a formal
statement to an empty room; Lin sees Chen rehearsing an apology to a filing
cabinet.

**Relationship turn:** Lin asks Chen why he keeps promising to handle things
instead of saying what he can actually do. Chen may answer honestly, deflect,
or make a practical offer. Attraction increases only if the answer is
specific and costs him status; no numeric romance shortcut is allowed.

**Hook:** the records box contains a 2014 renovation inventory whose entry
mentions a blue rabbit umbrella and red whistle.

### Scene 4 — “The Cost of Being Precise”

**Time/place:** Mar 17–18, distributed offices, dorm, and cloud archive.

Chen negotiates the accepted Ledger contract into explicit scope, deadlines,
and responsibility. Gao asks him privately to omit the timestamp edit until
after the fair. Lin asks for the private copy to be acknowledged. Luo sets the
last defensible permit-review deadline. Amani and Min-seo publish their access
and archive requirements.

**Central choice:** disclose now, disclose in a controlled meeting, or delay.
The Ledger does not choose. The harness must make the choice costly in
different ways: immediate friendship damage, greater institutional risk, or
reduced time for verification.

**Required subjective changes:** at least two evidence-linked belief updates
and one directed relationship change, with visible evidence preceding each.

**Hook:** Qiao sends Gao an instruction that is technically ambiguous but
timestamped: “Use the version we can defend.”

### Scene 5 — “The Person Who Asked for a Presentable Number”

**Time/place:** Mar 19, Riverbend Food Cooperative, bank-preparation room.

Xu, Qiao, Gao, He, and Chen confront the difference between a truthful
cash-flow summary and a misleading one. Xu reveals the loan pressure and her
conversation with Qiao. He reveals only enough of Gao's source relationship to
explain verification boundaries. Qiao can propose a lawful labeled preliminary
summary, accept responsibility, or pressure Gao to take sole blame.

**Comedy beat:** the Ledger rejects “make it look less alarming” as an
untyped action and offers three literal replacements: “explain,” “omit,” and
“mislabel.” Everyone is horrified that the supernatural entity has better
document vocabulary than they do.

**Hook:** the original cloud export proves the duplicate entry, but the edit
history shows a second account with access rights that no current participant
recognizes.

### Scene 6 — “The Blue Umbrella Is Not Evidence of Love”

**Time/place:** Mar 20, old neighborhood committee basement.

The group follows the inventory trail. They find the umbrella and reconstruct
the storm through records, not supernatural flashback. Lin learns that Chen
left to find an adult and returned with his aunt; Chen learns Lin waited alone
for twelve minutes and had been told not to discuss it.

Neither memory is declared false. The truth is that both children made a
reasonable interpretation from incomplete information and carried the cost for
thirteen years.

**Relationship choice:** Chen can apologize without demanding absolution;
Lin can state the harm without converting it into a permanent verdict; either
may decline further intimacy. The romance route advances only through mutual
consent after the difficult conversation.

**Cat beat:** the stray cat sleeps on the inventory box. The Ledger marks this
as “non-contractual occupancy” and cannot move it.

**Hook:** the unknown cloud account belongs to a former student-union archive
role, and Qiao knew the role existed but not who still had access.

### Scene 7 — “The Meeting Has Minutes”

**Time/place:** Mar 21–22, university review meeting.

Luo chairs a formal clarification meeting. All nine active characters have a
reason to attend or submit evidence. Min-seo locks the archive; Amani presents
access requirements; Xu presents the contract clause; He presents her evidence
standard; Lin presents the private copy; Gao admits the error and edit; Chen
admits holding the old form; Qiao explains his ambiguous instruction.

**No single confession solves the meeting.** Luo separates four findings:
financial error, records-integrity breach, unclear instruction, and permit
requirements. Each gets an owner and deadline. The unknown account is revoked
and preserved for an institutional audit, without accusing a named person
without evidence.

**Hook:** the fair permit can be conditionally restored, but only if the access
plan and corrected vendor reconciliation are both delivered by Mar 25 17:00.

### Scene 8 — “Everyone Is Busy at the Same Time”

**Time/place:** Mar 23–25, parallel preparation.

This is the pressure chapter. Agents pursue incompatible needs: Xu needs a
payment schedule; Amani needs a ramp and quiet room; Min-seo needs stable
documents; He needs a publishable fact pattern; Gao needs to hand over work;
Lin needs to stop personally checking every line; Qiao needs to decide whether
to remain; Luo needs a complete file and family time.

The harness may use routine macros, but it must interrupt agents on material
events: missed deadlines, unread messages, damaged equipment, changed weather,
or a person withdrawing cooperation. The cat may trigger one mundane delay,
never a miraculous rescue.

**Required turning point:** at least one preparation failure occurs and is
repaired by a person who bears a plausible cost. The fair must not succeed
because every plan works.

**Hook:** a rain warning moves the outdoor opening inside, threatening the
access plan and forcing a last-minute choice about what to cancel.

### Scene 9 — “The Fair Opens Under Conditional Approval”

**Time/place:** Mar 26, 08:00–21:30, community fair site.

The fair runs under a conditional permit. Chen is not the universal manager;
the named owners act. Amani can halt an inaccessible route. Xu can close the
stall if payment terms are breached. Min-seo can freeze the archive if files
are altered. He can publish only the verified portion. Luo can suspend a
component while preserving the rest. Qiao and Gao must perform the handover
they agreed to.

**Romance/comedy sequence:** Lin asks Chen to stay beside the boring records
table, explicitly because she wants company and not because the Ledger says so.
The Ledger labels this “voluntary co-presence, non-quantified.” Chen tries to
offer a contract; Lin confiscates his pen. The cat occupies the one chair
reserved for the compliance binder.

**Hook:** after closing, the Ledger reports that CASE 001 is not settled: one
term was satisfied in wording but not in mutually acknowledged scope.

### Scene 10 — “Settlement Is Not Forgiveness”

**Time/place:** Mar 27, 09:00–21:30, review office and riverside aftercare.

The group verifies the final reconciliation, access report, archive checksum,
and publication decision. Luo issues the permit completion record or a
documented partial closure. Gao signs the correction and role handover. Qiao
accepts review or resigns. Xu signs the payment schedule or closes the stall.
He publishes the correction-focused article only after the evidence threshold.

Chen must perform the final Ledger action: state what he will continue doing,
what he will stop doing, and which promise he cannot make. The Ledger offers
the bounded Objective Clarification reward only after all accepted conditions
are mutually acknowledged. Chen may ask about the recorded storm statement,
the cloud edit history, or the named document—not about Lin's feelings.

**Romantic resolution:** Lin offers a first meeting with no task attached (for
example, tea at the riverside market). Chen accepts without converting it into
a promise of forever. If either refuses, the ending remains emotionally
complete: honest boundaries are a valid terminal outcome.

**Final image:** the compliance folder is labeled `BORING PART — DO NOT FEED
THE CAT`. The cat immediately sits on it. The Ledger cannot classify this as a
breach because no human promised the cat anything.

## 6. Terminal states

The simulation must classify exactly one terminal state. These are objective
contracts, not prose labels.

### T-A: Conditional success / primary ending

All of the following must be true:

- CASE 001 is accepted and settled with every term acknowledged by its named
  participants.
- Corrected vendor reconciliation, cancellation explanation, named
  signatories, access plan, and archive checksum exist as authoritative
  documents.
- The fair either completes or every canceled component has a recorded owner,
  reason, and financial consequence.
- Gao's error and timestamp edit are disclosed; Chen's old-form concealment is
  disclosed; no innocent person is falsely accused.
- At least one formal consequence occurs: role loss, review, delayed payment,
  correction, or documented cancellation.
- Lin, Gao, Luo, He, Qiao, Xu, Amani, and Min-seo each have a terminal outcome
  other than “waiting.”
- At least four evidence-linked belief updates and two relationship-direction
  updates occur, including one non-romantic relationship.
- Chen and Lin either mutually agree to a non-obligatory future meeting or
  explicitly decline further romance without hostility.
- He's publication decision is evidence-calibrated and source boundaries are
  preserved.
- The Ledger grants only the stated bounded reward, never affection or luck.

### T-B: Honest failure / fair canceled

Valid when disclosure is honest but a hard constraint prevents the fair from
completing. It requires a documented cancellation, returned or rescheduled
funds, preserved evidence, named responsibility, and no fabricated approval.
The Ledger case may settle with a smaller social outcome, but it must not call
failure success. Character terminal outcomes and relationship updates remain
required.

### T-C: Institutional rupture

Valid when Qiao or another responsible party chooses cover-up and the evidence
eventually makes the permit or fair impossible. The endpoint requires the
cover-up's attributable evidence, formal suspension or resignation, source
protection, and a stable post-event state. No character may be forced to
forgive; Chen/Lin may separate.

### T-D: Personal withdrawal / unresolved case

Valid only if Chen refuses the Ledger or abandons the cluster. The fair and
institutional actors continue their own processes; the Ledger case remains
open and the refusal consequence is exactly the seeded one (no invented debt).
This is a terminal story ending only after the world advances to the fair's
decision and every character has a documented consequence.

## 7. Harness-facing execution requirements

These are requirements for the later implementation, not invitations for a GM
to fill gaps:

- Scene boundaries are projections of authoritative event predicates. A judge
  may format or classify an unresolved interaction, but may not declare a
  chapter complete.
- Every important decision has a typed intention, actor, observed evidence,
  precondition, cost, resulting event, and downstream affordance change.
- The world must run to a terminal predicate, not to a fixed number of turns.
  A safety ceiling may abort as `invalid/incomplete`, never as success.
- Routine schedules should be compressed into macros only when they have no
  unresolved social choice and no hidden material event. Macro expansion must
  leave an auditable event range.
- Private evidence remains recipient-scoped. A character cannot act on a
  secret merely because it exists in the seed or in another agent's trace.
- Mental state changes require visible evidence and must record the evidence
  event version. Relationship changes are directed and typed (trust,
  competence, warmth, resentment, reliance), not one romance score.
- The Ledger's system-agent output is a proposal constrained by the accepted
  contract. It cannot create a new NPC motivation, reveal an arbitrary secret,
  or repair an unsuccessful plan.
- The semantic judge is invoked only for genuinely unresolved social meaning;
  its output must be a bounded typed proposal accepted or rejected by the
  kernel. The complete arc must be able to terminate without a judge inventing
  plot.

## 8. Quality gates for calling the run complete

Do not report completion merely because a JSON file exists or because the
clock reaches Mar 27. A run is publishable as a demo only when:

1. The terminal classifier returns one of T-A through T-D and all predicates
   for that state pass.
2. The authoritative event log is contiguous, replayable, and sufficient to
   reconstruct objective state.
3. Every active character has at least one independent decision and a terminal
   outcome; no one is silently teleported into cooperation.
4. The VN projection contains ten ordered chapters, scene entrances/exits,
   dialogue/action beats, choices with consequences, and a final resolution
   record. Routine waits do not inflate the scene count.
5. The trace contains no privacy leak, impossible movement, stale-version
   commit, unexplained supernatural effect, or judge-created fact.
6. The causal report can answer, for every major turn: what the actor knew,
   what they wanted, what they chose, what it cost, and which later state it
   changed.
7. The displayed ending describes the actual terminal state. If the fair is
   canceled, the VN must say so; if Chen and Lin decline romance, it must not
   render a confession.
8. A human reading the VN projection can identify the opening problem, three
   escalating reversals, the emotional reveal, the climax, the consequences,
   and the final image without reading the raw event log.

## 9. Implementation sequence after approval

1. Convert the canonical spine and terminal predicates into seed data and
   immutable identifiers; do not yet add prose generation.
2. Add explicit objectives, commitments, evidence objects, document versions,
   deadlines, and role ownership to the world state.
3. Add primitive actions needed by the ten scenes: inspect, disclose,
   acknowledge, sign, publish, revoke-access, schedule, cancel-component,
   handover, and request-consent. Keep each typed and auditable.
4. Replace the current repetitive policies with goal-driven policies that can
   pursue competing outcomes and refuse requests for plausible reasons.
5. Implement scene predicates and terminal classification before extending the
   VN prose projection.
6. Run a cheap deterministic dry run with full trace and reject it on any
   incomplete terminal predicate. Do not call this the story run.
7. Run the expensive full multi-agent simulation through the terminal event,
   inspect both the rendered VN projection and the causal audit, then iterate
   within v1 until one complete terminal is genuinely coherent.

The first successful full run should be reported with the rendered ten-scene
story and its terminal classification, not only event counts.
