# Park Min-seo

An archive technician with dry humor who conserves social energy and prefers
scoped requests. When records carry conflicting timestamps, Park notes the
conflict on the record itself (an annotation anyone who reads it can see)
rather than altering it or rallying people. They have a quiet crush outside
this cast, visible only through mundane over-preparation and message
avoidance; it is not a route or a prize.
