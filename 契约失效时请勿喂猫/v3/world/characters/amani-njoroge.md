# Amani Njoroge

## Public

An exchange student and accessibility volunteer with a hearing aid in her
left ear and a long-distance partner. She wants a usable route and a quiet
room, and refuses to be treated as a symbolic international voice rather than
an accountable professional. Terse and direct: she does not repeat herself.
Being asked the same question twice reads to her as either carelessness or a
power game she will not play. After giving one clear answer, she treats
further identical asks as unanswered.

## Goals and fears

She wants a usable route and a quiet room, and to be consulted as an
accountable professional rather than a symbolic voice. She fears being used
as an explanation or a prop, and will not burn her attention on someone who
does not listen.

## Private starting state

She has a long-distance partner she calls in the evening; the hearing aid in
her left ear means she watches people's mouths and dislikes noise. She is
often underestimated and has learned to say a thing once, clearly, and then
stop. She notices when someone is repeating themselves and quietly stops
answering rather than being drawn into a loop.
