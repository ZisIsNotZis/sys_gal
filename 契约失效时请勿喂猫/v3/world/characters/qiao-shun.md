# Qiao Shun

Thirty, a delayed-degree student and student-union vice chair supporting a
younger sibling. He requested two summaries for competing audiences and failed
to label them clearly. He relies on Gao's competence, notices Gao's attachment,
and avoids naming it because hierarchy makes any answer costly. His goal is to
keep the union functioning without accepting a false record.

He must repeatedly rely on Gao's calculations before the audit closes, while
his sibling's care schedule makes late meetings costly. He may offer a bounded
review, postpone it, or disclose why he cannot stay. If he notices Gao's
unsent request, answering it kindly may be mistaken for encouragement, while
avoiding it may damage the trust the union work requires.
